V Session

How a steam session with me works.

Up to 60°C heat

Deep warming — the body has time to absorb it fully.

Pure water on hot stones

Soft, breathing steam that nourishes.

Good ventilation

Fresh air and steady breathing throughout the whole session.

Gradual, layered warming

The body opens layer by layer.

Oak whisk work

Lymph moves, fascia softens, heat goes deeper.

Meditative practices and voice

The mind anchors in the present, relaxation goes deeper.

— how the rounds unfold —
Before the rounds ·

Opening circle

We meet, settle in, I explain what's ahead. A moment to arrive and attune before the warmth begins.

Round 1 01

Warm-up

Getting used to the heat, first sweat, attention turns inward — returning to connection with the body.

Rounds 2–4 02–04

Whisk work

Oak whisks move across the body in the heat. Tissues open, lymph flows, breathing slows. By the final round, the nervous system reaches a state deeper than sleep.

VII My path

How I came to steam.

— steam found me in 2020

Origin

Vladivostok · PhD

Born in Vladivostok. PhD in experimental physics. That scientific foundation stayed with me — it shapes how I work with the body in the steam.

15 years

Rhythmic gymnastics

15 years in sport. I learned a great deal through the body. Today, attention to sensation and respect for boundaries matter deeply to me.

Career

Deloitte · Sustainability

Strategy consulting at Deloitte, Sustainability practice. Experience working with structure and complex challenges — now useful in thermal space consulting.

2020

First ceremonial steam

It happened at a regional Burning Man in 2020. I walked into a ceremonial steam space and never left. Haven't stopped since.

2020 —

5 years, 7 countries

Steamed in Russia, Sweden, the Netherlands, Portugal, Poland, Lithuania, Germany. Hundreds of guests. Camp lead of Banya Alpacas and NII Banya, worked at Waking Life and regional Burning Man.

Now

Body · IFS · 350+ hours

Over 350 hours of somatic practice — steam, massage, somatic therapy. I practise IFS — using elements of the method in the steam to work with mental load.
